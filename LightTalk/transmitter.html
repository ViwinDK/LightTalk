💡 LightTalk Beacon

This laptop becomes the transmitter. The square will flash your message as light using the LightTalk v1 protocol — point the iPhone's Decode tab at it and tap the square on the phone screen.

⚠️ Before starting: screen brightness to maximum; turn off Night Shift, True Tone and auto-brightness; make the browser fullscreen (F11 on Windows, green button on Mac); keep this tab focused. Note: the square flashes ~2.5×/sec — look away if sensitive to flashing light.

No network, no pairing — just light. Same bits the Arduino sends.
